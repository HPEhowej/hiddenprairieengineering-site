Built Around How Cities Actually Work

Engineering informed by real municipal experience.

Hidden Prairie Engineering is a focused civil engineering practice based in Southeast Iowa. Our approach combines technical design experience with an understanding of the operational, financial, and administrative challenges communities face.

Clients work directly with a principal engineer who understands public works operations, council expectations, funding requirements, and the coordination necessary to move projects from initial planning through construction.

The result is straightforward communication, efficient project delivery, and engineering solutions that make sense both on paper and in the field.
