Built Around How Cities Actually Work

Experience on both the design and operations side of municipal projects.

Hidden Prairie Engineering is a small, focused civil engineering practice based in Southeast Iowa, supporting communities and clients across the region. The goal is simple: provide practical engineering help that respects budgets, schedules, and the realities of day-to-day public works operations.

You work directly with a principal engineer who understands council expectations, funding requirements, and the coordination needed to move projects from idea to construction.

That means fewer surprises, clearer decisions, and designs that move smoothly from planning to construction.
